Privacy summary

Designed around lightweight interaction

Mini Ritual Spinner provides AI-selected ritual tasks, visual glow states, and pulse feedback through a compact vertical interface. The extension does not require a dedicated personal account for its standard task-cycling functionality.

01

Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install, access, or use the Mini Ritual Spinner browser extension.

Mini Ritual Spinner is a compact task-cycling extension that presents ritual-style activities through a vertical toolbar popup interface.

Tasks may be selected or ordered using AI-related logic and are presented with glowing highlights and pulse feedback to indicate the current position in the cycle.

This Privacy Policy applies to Mini Ritual Spinner itself and does not govern unrelated websites, browser vendors, operating systems, or independent third-party services.

02

Single Purpose

Mini Ritual Spinner has a focused purpose: allowing users to quickly cycle through small ritual tasks in a compact browser toolbar popup.

Functionality reasonably related to this purpose may include:

  • Displaying a vertical task list;
  • Selecting ritual tasks;
  • Highlighting the current task;
  • Moving to the next task;
  • Displaying glow effects;
  • Displaying pulse feedback;
  • Resetting or restarting a ritual cycle;
  • Maintaining temporary task state; and
  • Other controls directly related to task cycling.
03

No Account Required

Mini Ritual Spinner does not require users to create a dedicated account in order to use its standard functionality.

Users do not need to register a username, password, personal profile, or email address simply to view and cycle through ritual tasks.

04

Ritual Tasks

Mini Ritual Spinner displays short task prompts intended to provide a lightweight sequence of activities within the extension interface.

Ritual tasks may include simple prompts relating to organization, reflection, focus, creativity, routine, or other general-purpose activities supported by the extension.

Tasks are provided as part of the extension experience and are not intended to represent professional advice.

05

Task Cycling

Users may move through available ritual tasks in sequence.

The extension may process temporary functional information such as:

Current task Identifies which ritual is currently highlighted.
Task position Used to determine where the user is within the current cycle.
Previous task May be used to maintain interface continuity while cycling.
Next task Used to determine the next available ritual in the sequence.
06

Glowing Highlights

Mini Ritual Spinner uses visual highlights to indicate which task is currently active.

Functional values used to render glowing feedback may include visual state, intensity, active-item position, animation progress, or other presentation values.

These values are used for interface rendering and are not inherently personally identifiable information.

07

Pulse Feedback

The extension may use pulse animations to provide visual feedback as users move between ritual tasks.

Pulse effects may rely on temporary values such as timing, animation state, transition progress, and the currently active task.

These values are processed only to support the extension interface.

08

AI-Selected Tasks

Mini Ritual Spinner may use AI-related selection logic to determine which ritual tasks are presented or how available tasks are ordered.

AI selection does not automatically imply personal profiling.

The extension's stated purpose is to choose and present ritual tasks. Task selection is not intended to create advertising, credit, employment, insurance, or other unrelated user profiles.

The specific technical implementation of task selection may evolve over time. If a future version materially changes how user information is processed in connection with AI functionality, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

09

Standard task cycling does not require information such as a user's legal name, home address, government identifier, financial credentials, or private account password.

AI-related task selection is intended to support the extension's disclosed ritual-task functionality.
